Affordable Housing Projects

Browse affordable housing projects built or preserved by HPD across NYC — filter by borough, unit size, and income tier. This shows the affordable housing supply, not current lottery listings. To apply to a specific lottery, use NYC Housing Connect ↗ — that's where open lotteries and their application deadlines live.

Area Median Income (AMI) tiers set eligibility — a household of 4 at 80% AMI in NYC is roughly $130K/year; 30% AMI is roughly $49K. Each project reports how many units it dedicates to each tier — a project with 100 "extremely low" units means it must rent 100 apartments only to eligible low-income households.
